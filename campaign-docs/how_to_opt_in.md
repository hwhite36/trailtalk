# TrailTalk Beta - Access & Opt-In Guide

**TrailTalk** is an automated, SMS-based outdoor assistant software application that provides real-time weather reports, navigational support, and backcountry survival information via text message. 

This page outlines the official Call-to-Action (CTA) and opt-in instructions for our closed-beta software deployment.

---

## How to Join the Closed Beta
Access to TrailTalk is currently restricted to authorized beta testers. Because this is a private software deployment, access to this beta is subject to the administrator's discretion. 

1. Request to join the beta by creating a [**GitHub Issue**](https://github.com/hwhite36/trailtalk/issues) on this repository containing why you want to join the beta, an email address to get in touch with you, and an acknowledgement that you recognize that this product is a closed beta and that you will not share access with anyone else.
2. The administrator will review your request and reply to your GitHub issue indicating if your request has been approved or denied.
3. If your request is approved by the administrator, you will receive an email to the address you provided containing the closed beta passphrase and TrailTalk phone number.
4. Text the provided passphrase to the provided number, thereby completing your opt-in to the beta, and ensure you receive an immediate response.
5. If you have any issues, follow up on your GitHub Issue (or raise a new one) and the administrator will get in touch with you with further guidance.

---

## Sample Opt-In & Message Flow

When you register via SMS, the system responds with the following mandatory disclosures:

* **User Texts:** `[Authorized Beta Passphrase]`
* **TrailTalk Replies:** 
  > "Welcome! You are now registered. Reply to begin chatting. By replying, you agree to receive text messages from TrailTalk. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help."

---

## Managing Your Preferences & Support

* **Opt-Out / Cancellation:** You can cancel or unsubscribe from messages at any time by replying **STOP** (or **UNSUBSCRIBE**) to any message. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.
* **Help / Support:** For assistance with the service, you can reply **HELP** or visit our public repository support tracker: [TrailTalk GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hwhite36/trailtalk/issues).
