### Privacy Policy for TrailTalk

**Effective Date:**  October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with this closed-beta deployment managed by the independent developer / repository administrator of TrailTalk, an automated SMS assistant service designed for outdoor navigation, weather updates, and backcountry support. In this document, "we," "us," or "our" refers specifically to the independent developer hosting this private, closed-beta instance of the app.

##### 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with TrailTalk via SMS, we collect the following information:

* **Mobile Phone Number:**  Collected automatically when you text our service.

* **Message Content:**  The text queries, location coordinates, or other messages you send to the assistant.

* **Usage & Logs:**  Basic message metadata (timestamps and delivery statuses) necessary for maintaining system reliability.

##### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

* Provide AI-generated assistant responses, weather updates, and backcountry information.

* Maintain conversation history so the assistant can contextualize follow-up questions.

* Ensure application security, prevent abuse, and enforce registration passphrases.

##### 3. Mobile Information & SMS Opt-In Consent (Strict Non-Sharing Policy)

**We respect your privacy.**  Mobile information, phone numbers, and SMS opt-in consent will **NOT** be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

To further specify and make abundantly clear, we do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

##### 4. Data Storage and Security

Conversation records and user data are securely stored in our backend database, which operates as a parallel PostgreSQL Docker container deployed on personally managed, self-hosted infrastructure rather than a corporate cloud environment. We employ standard technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

##### 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize infrastructure providers (such as Twilio for SMS processing and Google Gemini for AI responses) solely to execute the core operations of the application. These providers process data strictly as needed to deliver our service and are bound by confidentiality standards.

##### 6. Your Rights & Opting Out

You can stop receiving messages or remove your number from our active service at any time by replying **STOP** to any message.
